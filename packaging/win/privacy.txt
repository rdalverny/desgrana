Desgrana works offline. It reads your session files and writes tracks to your
disk. Nothing is uploaded.

The only network request is an optional update check, once a month, to:

    https://romaindalverny.com/desgrana/version.json

It sends: operating system, OS version, CPU architecture, Desgrana version and
system language. Nothing else.

It is off by default. You can turn it on on the next page ("Check for updates").

Full privacy policy: https://github.com/rdalverny/desgrana/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
